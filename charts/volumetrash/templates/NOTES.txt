volumetrash {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

It purges trashed volumes (PersistentVolumeClaims) once their retention has
passed. Retention comes from the owning project's trashRetention, else from the
platform config in {{ .Values.platform.namespace }}/{{ .Values.platform.configMap }}.

It needs the Project CRD (project.wutz.dev) to be installed in the cluster.

Check the operator:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get deployment {{ include "volumetrash.fullname" . }}
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deployment/{{ include "volumetrash.fullname" . }}
